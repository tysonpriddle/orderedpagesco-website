Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Ordered Pages Co ("we", "us", "our") operates the website orderedpagesco.com and sells digital products via Etsy, Gumroad, and other platforms. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate our business:

  • Purchase information: When you buy a product through Etsy or Gumroad, those platforms collect your name, email, and payment details on our behalf. We do not receive your payment details directly. We may receive your name and email address to fulfil your order.
  • Communications: If you contact us by email, we retain the content of that correspondence.
  • Website analytics: We may collect anonymised visitor data (page views, referral source) via standard analytics tools. No personally identifying information is stored.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfil digital product orders and deliver download links.
  • To respond to customer enquiries.
  • To improve our products and shop experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, do not sell it to third parties, and do not share it with anyone except where required to fulfil your order.

3. Third-Party Platforms

Our products are sold via Etsy and Gumroad. Each platform has its own privacy policy which governs the data they collect. We also use Pinterest to share content publicly — Pinterest's privacy policy applies to interactions on their platform.

Our automation tools (used internally to publish content) access only our own business accounts on these platforms and do not collect or process any customer data.

4. Data Retention

We retain purchase-related information only as long as necessary for customer support and legal compliance. Email correspondence is retained for up to 2 years.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

6. Cookies

This website may use essential cookies for basic functionality. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at:
orderedpagesco@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be published at this URL with the date of last update shown above.
