About Ordered Pages Co

Minimal templates for paperless people

Our Story

Why We Exist

Ordered Pages Co started with a simple frustration: the best digital paper devices in the world — reMarkable, Supernote, BOOX — come with very few useful templates out of the box. The templates that exist are either buried in forums, locked behind paywalls, or designed for A4 printers rather than e-ink screens.

We set out to fix that. Every template we make is designed specifically for the pixel dimensions and contrast requirements of e-ink displays. They are clean, minimal, and functional — no decorative clutter, no wasted space.

All 30 templates are free. No email list, no account, no upsell. Just download and use.

Our Approach

Design Philosophy

We design for the device first. That means understanding how e-ink renders grey tones, where the finger or stylus naturally rests on screen, and how much whitespace actually helps when you are writing by hand rather than typing.

Templates are exported at 1404×1872px — native reMarkable 2 resolution. They scale correctly to Paper Pro, Supernote A5X, and BOOX devices. When used in GoodNotes or Notability on iPad, they appear at full quality on an A4-sized canvas.

Free by default

All core templates are free and always will be. We believe useful tools should be accessible, not gated.

No tracking

We do not run ads or tracking pixels. Download links go straight to the file. No analytics on your behaviour.

Device-first design

Every dimension, line weight, and grey value is tested on actual hardware before release.

Honest copy

We will tell you what each template is genuinely useful for and what it is not. No marketing fluff.

The Collection

What We Make

Thirty templates across three categories: Budget & Finance, Planners, and Wellness. Each category contains ten standalone templates that work on their own without needing the others.

The budget templates cover everything from weekly spending to annual planning, debt payoff, and subscription tracking. The planner templates include daily and weekly pages, meeting notes, goal worksheets, and habit trackers. The wellness templates span mood, sleep, nutrition, mindfulness, and self-care.

We plan to expand to 60+ templates over 2026. Upcoming categories include study and focus, project management, and travel planning.
